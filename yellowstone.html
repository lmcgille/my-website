Yellowstone

The World's First National Park

Established in 1872, Yellowstone National Park is a nearly 3,500-sq.-mile wilderness recreation area atop a volcanic hot spot. Mostly in Wyoming, the park spreads into Montana and Idaho too. Yellowstone features dramatic canyons, alpine rivers, lush forests, hot springs and gushing geysers, including its most famous, Old Faithful.

It's also home to hundreds of animal species, including bears, wolves, bison, elk and antelope. The park's varied ecosystems provide endless opportunities for hiking, camping, and wildlife photography.
