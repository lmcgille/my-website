Banff

Canada's First National Park

Banff National Park is Canada's oldest national park, established in 1885. Located in the Alberta Rockies, it encompasses 6,641 square kilometres of mountainous terrain, with many glaciers and ice fields, dense coniferous forest, and alpine landscapes.

Banff is renowned for its turquoise glacial lakes, including Lake Louise and Moraine Lake, set against a backdrop of rugged peaks. The park offers world-class hiking, skiing, and hot springs, making it a premier destination for outdoor enthusiasts year-round.
